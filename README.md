# Awesome Self-Publishing [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of tools, services and resources for indie authors and self-publishers, from first draft to first sale.

Self-publishing means doing a publisher's whole job yourself: writing, editing, formatting, cover design, ISBNs, distribution and marketing. This list collects the tools and resources that indie authors actually use at each step.

Curated by [Rocking Book Covers](https://www.rockingbookcovers.com), a book cover design studio for self-publishing authors. Suggestions are welcome: see [Contributing](#contributing).

## Writing and Planning

- [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) - Long-form writing app for organizing chapters, research and notes in one project.
- [Plottr](https://plottr.com) - Visual plotting and series-planning tool with timelines and story templates.
- [Reedsy Studio](https://reedsy.com/studio) - Free online writing and book-formatting editor.

## Editing and Proofreading

- [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com) - Grammar and style checker with in-depth reports built for fiction writers.
- [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com) - Grammar, spelling and clarity checker that works in most apps.
- [Hemingway Editor](https://hemingwayapp.com) - Highlights long sentences, passive voice and hard-to-read passages.
- [Reedsy Marketplace](https://reedsy.com) - Marketplace for vetted freelance editors, proofreaders and other publishing professionals.
- [Editorial Freelancers Association](https://www.the-efa.org) - Directory of professional editors, plus guidance on typical editing rates.

## Formatting and Ebook Tools

- [Vellum](https://vellum.pub) - Mac app that turns a manuscript into polished ebook and print interiors.
- [Atticus](https://www.atticus.io) - Cross-platform writing and formatting tool for ebook and print, an alternative to Vellum.
- [Calibre](https://calibre-ebook.com) - Free, open-source ebook manager and converter.
- [Sigil](https://sigil-ebook.com) - Free, open-source EPUB editor for hand-tuning ebook files.
- [EPUBCheck](https://www.w3.org/publishing/epubcheck/) - Official validator for EPUB files. Catch errors before retailers reject your book.

## Book Covers

- [Book Cover Specs](https://github.com/rockingbookcovers/book-cover-specs) - Cheat sheet of cover sizes for KDP, IngramSpark and ebook stores, with the spine-width formula and a print-ready checklist.
- [KDP Cover Calculator](https://kdp.amazon.com/cover-calculator) - Amazon's official tool for full-wrap paperback and hardcover cover templates.
- [IngramSpark Cover File Creation](https://help.ingramspark.com/hc/en-us/articles/35306346735245-Cover-File-Creation) - IngramSpark's cover requirements and template generator.
- Lousy Book Covers (https://www.lousybookcovers.com) - a collection of bad book covers and criticisms of them, useful stuff to learn 
- [Rocking Book Covers](https://www.rockingbookcovers.com) - Custom book cover design and 3,000+ exclusive premade covers across every genre.

## ISBNs and Identifiers

- [International ISBN Agency](https://www.isbn-international.org/agencies) - Find the official ISBN agency for your country.
- [Bowker MyIdentifiers](https://www.myidentifiers.com) - Official ISBN agency for the United States, also sells barcodes.
- [Nielsen ISBN Store](https://www.nielsenisbnstore.com) - Official ISBN agency for the UK and Ireland.

## Ebook and Print Distribution

- [Amazon KDP](https://kdp.amazon.com) - Publish ebooks, paperbacks and hardcovers on Amazon.
- [IngramSpark](https://www.ingramspark.com) - Print-on-demand and ebook distribution to bookstores and libraries worldwide.
- [Draft2Digital](https://www.draft2digital.com) - Ebook and print distribution to many retailers and library platforms from one dashboard. Smashwords is now part of Draft2Digital.
- [Kobo Writing Life](https://www.kobo.com/writinglife) - Publish directly to the Kobo ebook and audiobook store.
- [Apple Books for Authors](https://authors.apple.com) - Publish directly to Apple Books.
- [Google Play Books Partner Center](https://play.google.com/books/publish/) - Publish directly to Google Play Books.
- [Barnes & Noble Press](https://press.barnesandnoble.com) - Publish ebooks and print books directly to Barnes & Noble.
- [PublishDrive](https://publishdrive.com) - Ebook, print and audiobook distribution with subscription-based pricing.

## Audiobooks

- [ACX](https://www.acx.com) - Produce and distribute audiobooks to Audible, Amazon and Apple.
- [Findaway Voices by Spotify](https://findawayvoices.com) - Wide audiobook distribution to retailers and libraries.

## Direct Sales and Reader Delivery

- [BookFunnel](https://bookfunnel.com) - Deliver ebooks and audiobooks to readers for reader magnets, advance copies and direct sales.
- [Payhip](https://payhip.com) - Sell ebooks and other digital products directly from your own store.

## Marketing and Promotion

- [BookBub Partners](https://partners.bookbub.com) - Featured Deals, ads and pre-order alerts for reaching BookBub's readers.
- [Written Word Media](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com) - Paid ebook promotions through newsletters such as Freebooksy and Bargain Booksy.
- [Publisher Rocket](https://publisherrocket.com) - Keyword, category and competition research for Amazon books.
- [StoryOrigin](https://storyoriginapp.com) - Newsletter swaps, group promotions and reader-magnet delivery for authors.

## Reviews and Advance Readers

- [NetGalley](https://www.netgalley.com) - Get advance copies into the hands of reviewers, librarians and booksellers.
- [Booksprout](https://booksprout.co) - Find and manage advance-review readers.
- [Goodreads Author Program](https://www.goodreads.com/author/program) - Claim your author profile and run giveaways on Goodreads.

## Learning and Communities

- [Alliance of Independent Authors (ALLi)](https://www.allianceindependentauthors.org) - Professional association for self-publishing authors, with guides and a vetted services directory.
- [Kindlepreneur](https://kindlepreneur.com) - Free guides on Amazon publishing, book marketing and author tools.
- [The Creative Penn](https://www.thecreativepenn.com) - Joanna Penn's blog and podcast on writing and the business of self-publishing.
- [Jane Friedman](https://janefriedman.com) - In-depth articles on the publishing industry and author careers.
- [r/selfpublish](https://www.reddit.com/r/selfpublish/) - Large Reddit community for questions about every step of self-publishing.

## Avoiding Scams and Protecting Your Rights

- [Writer Beware](https://writerbeware.blog) - Warnings about publishing scams, predatory services and bad contracts.
- [U.S. Copyright Office: Registration](https://www.copyright.gov/registration/) - Register the copyright for your book in the United States.
